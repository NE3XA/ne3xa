NE3XA achievement badges

Earn a badge when you score A or B on a language final exam. Badges recognise real skill — not just course completion.

How to earn a badge

  1. Complete the relevant course materials
  2. Sit the timed online final, or download the paper and submit by the deadline
  3. Score Grade A (80%+) or Grade B (65–79%)
  4. Your badge is recorded and can be shared on your profile or CV
